## ✨ Features

- **Responsive & Modern Design:** Crafted with high aesthetic standards, responsive layouts, smooth scroll, and glassmorphism elements.
- **Brand Showcase:** Monogram logo and official brand storytelling ("Made for Everyday").
- **Product Spotlight:** Full breakdown of the **CUET-inspired 750 mL Premium Aluminium Water Bottle**.
- **Interactive Gallery & Image Switcher:** Multi-angle product photos, unboxing mockup, real-life batch arrival shots, and lifestyle visuals.
- **Pre-Order Flow & Payment Instructions:** Seamless pre-order steps with exact advance/remaining price breakdown, bKash/Nagad instructions, and direct Google Form link.
- **Dual Contact Channels:** Dedicated contact cards for both the founder (Mahirul) and secondary contact/feedback liaison (Hossain).
- **Custom Favicon & Tab Branding:** Browser tab icon generated directly from the official **MH** emblem.
